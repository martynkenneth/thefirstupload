Kids Diana Show is a family channel built around Diana and her older brother Roma. Their role-play adventures for young children made it one of the most-viewed channels in the world.

## Video one
The channel launched in 2015. Its first video is a short greeting vlog of Diana as a toddler, with a title in both Russian and English.

## The breakthrough
Short, playful stories about everyday situations, with little dialogue and lots of expression, made the videos easy to follow for children everywhere. The family expanded into several channels and many languages.

## Today
Kids Diana Show runs multiple channels and a wider brand that includes animated series and toys, and remains one of the most-subscribed channels on YouTube.
