Alan Becker is an animator best known for Animator vs. Animation, a series in which a stick figure fights back against the person drawing him.

## Video one
The oldest video on his channel is "Trampoline time-lapse" from December 2006, a home video from long before his animations took over.

## The breakthrough
Animator vs. Animation first spread as a Flash animation in 2006 and became an internet classic. On YouTube, the series grew into a wordless, emotional world of stick figures on a computer desktop, including the hugely popular Animation vs. Minecraft and Animation vs. Math episodes.

## Today
Becker and his team continue the series, along with educational videos and the Alan Becker Tutorials channel for aspiring animators.
