TheDooo is a guitarist and producer known for playing guitar to strangers in online games and on Omegle, and for showcasing his skill.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Playing Guitar on Black Ops 2 Ep. 1 - Entertaining Randoms" from September 2013, which shows the idea that made him famous.

## The breakthrough
Surprising strangers with incredible guitar playing, first in game lobbies and then on video chat, made his videos hugely shareable.

## Today
He introduces himself simply: "Hey, I'm Eric. I play games and produce music."
