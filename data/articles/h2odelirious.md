H2ODelirious is a gaming creator famous for his infectious, high-pitched laugh and his hockey-mask avatar, as part of VanossGaming's group of friends.

## Video one
His oldest public video, from May 2011, is a Call of Duty: Black Ops "funtage", a mix of funny moments and gameplay.

## The breakthrough
His comedic gaming videos, especially group sessions with Vanoss and friends in GTA V and other games, made him one of the best-known faces of the Vanoss crew. He famously kept his face hidden from fans for years.

## Today
He still uploads gaming videos, with the stated aim of making everyone "laugh and feel happy throughout their day".
