IShowSpeed, or just Speed, is one of the most-watched livestreamers in the world. He is known for loud, unpredictable reactions, his devotion to Cristiano Ronaldo, and live streams that turn city streets into chaos.

## Video one
He registered his channel in 2016, and the oldest video still on it is a 2017 upload simply titled "{2k18} {First Video}". Early content was gaming, made while he was still in high school.

## The breakthrough
Speed's audience exploded in 2021, when clips of his explosive reactions while gaming spread across TikTok and YouTube Shorts. In 2022 he moved toward football and general entertainment, and released the single "World Cup", which charted in several countries.

## Today
Since 2024 Speed has livestreamed tours across several continents, drawing huge crowds and international news coverage. Many viewers credit those streams with showing everyday life in countries that rarely appear on mainstream American media.
