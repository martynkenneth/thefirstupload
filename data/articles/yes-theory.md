Yes Theory is a group of friends who make adventure and travel videos around one motto: "Seek Discomfort".

## Video one
The channel started out as Generation Y Not. Its oldest public video is "Yugo Strong" from March 2015.

## The breakthrough
Yes Theory first got national attention in November 2015 with a message of inclusivity after the terror attacks in Beirut and Paris. Videos of saying "yes" to strangers, unusual challenges and trips around the world built a large, loyal audience.

## Today
Their motto is also the name of their clothing brand, Seek Discomfort, and they believe "life's greatest moments and deepest connections exist outside your comfort zone".
