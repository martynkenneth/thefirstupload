Jordan Matter is a photographer who became a YouTube star by mixing photography with dance, gymnastics and challenge videos, often featuring his daughter.

## Video one
His oldest public video, "Jordan Matter Headshots" from February 2010, comes from his career as a professional photographer.

## The breakthrough
Matter was already known for his photography book Dancers Among Us. On YouTube he found a huge audience with challenge videos featuring dancers and gymnasts, built around creative photo shoots and flexibility stunts.

## Today
His channel focuses on challenges with young dancers and athletes. As his channel description puts it, "My daughter has taken over my channel!"
