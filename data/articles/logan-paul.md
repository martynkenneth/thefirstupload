Logan Paul went from Vine star to vlogger, podcaster, boxer and WWE wrestler. He is one of the most recognisable, and most debated, creators of his generation.

## Video one
Logan first found fame on Vine, the six-second video app, in 2013. His Logan Paul Vlogs channel launched in 2015, and its oldest public upload is "MY FIRST VLOG - THE ROAD TO BURNING MAN!" from September 2016. YouTube has since age-restricted it.

## The breakthrough
Daily vlogging made him one of the highest-paid YouTubers in 2017. In early 2018 he faced widespread criticism over a video filmed in Japan, which he removed and apologised for. He later moved into podcasting with Impaulsive and into boxing.

## Today
Logan has wrestled for WWE since 2022, winning the United States Championship, and co-founded the Prime drinks brand with KSI. The Impaulsive podcast remains one of his main outlets.
