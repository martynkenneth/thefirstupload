Like Nastya is a children's channel featuring Nastya and her parents, playing, learning and exploring the world together. It is one of the most-subscribed channels on YouTube.

## Video one
The channel's oldest video, from December 2016, is a family travel vlog in Russian about riding elephants and feeding fish, filmed when Nastya was a toddler.

## The breakthrough
The family turned to short, cheerful videos about colours, songs, numbers and good habits, and dubbed them into many languages. That made the channel a favourite with young children worldwide.

## Today
Like Nastya runs channels in many languages, including the spin-off Learn Like Nastya, and has grown into a wider children's brand.
