Jeffree Star is a makeup artist and entrepreneur who founded Jeffree Star Cosmetics. He has also been a musician and a beauty YouTuber.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "JEFFREE STAR SPEAKING JUST THE FACTS..." from April 2009, from his early years as a MySpace-era musician and personality.

## The breakthrough
Star released the album Beauty Killer in 2009 and toured internationally. He founded Jeffree Star Cosmetics in 2014, and his blunt, glamorous product reviews made his channel one of the most watched in beauty. Forbes listed him among the highest-paid YouTubers in 2018.

## Today
He runs his cosmetics company and has returned to music, announcing a re-release of Beauty Killer in 2024.
