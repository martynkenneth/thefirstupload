Andre Rebelo, known as Typical Gamer, is a Canadian creator and streamer who has been making gaming videos since the late 2000s.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Red Dead Redemption - Free For All (34-9)" from May 2010, a multiplayer match from the game that first grew his channel.

## The breakthrough
Red Dead Redemption and then Grand Theft Auto made Typical Gamer popular, and he became one of the early big names in livestreaming on YouTube.

## Today
He streams and uploads daily, and in 2024 co-founded Jogo Games, a studio that builds games inside Fortnite.
