Lilly Singh, once known online as IISuperwomanII, is a Canadian comedian who went from YouTube sketches to hosting an American late-night talk show.

## Video one
Her oldest public video is "How to Tie a Side Turla Bhangra Pagh (Turban)" from December 2010, a tutorial from the start of her YouTube journey.

## The breakthrough
Singh's sketches about her Punjabi parents and everyday life became hugely popular in the early 2010s. She wrote the bestselling book How to Be a Bawse.

## Today
From 2019 to 2021 she hosted NBC's A Little Late with Lilly Singh, becoming the first person of Indian descent to host an American late-night show. She also voices Tiffany Fluffit in The Bad Guys films.
