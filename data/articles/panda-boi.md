Federico Hu, known as Panda Boi, is an Italian-Chinese creator and actor who became one of Italy's most popular YouTubers with street interviews and viral short videos.

## Video one
He created his channel in 2020. Its oldest public video, from December 2020, is a Short about trying to give flowers to strangers.

## The breakthrough
Panda Boi started with street interviews, then created short-video trends that were copied by creators in Italy and abroad. In 2024 Fortune Italy listed him among its top 25 content creators.

## Today
In 2025 he won the Grand Prix at the WIBA Awards in Cannes. His channel states his goal plainly: "MY DREAM IS TO REACH 100 MILLION SUBSCRIBERS!"
