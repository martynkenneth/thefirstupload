Kara and Nate are a married couple from Nashville who started a YouTube channel to document a year of travel. That year turned into four.

## Video one
Their oldest public video, "world travels so far." from January 2016, comes from the start of their journey.

## The breakthrough
Their friendly travel vlogs found a loyal audience. At the end of 2019 they visited their 100th country.

## Today
They continue to make travel videos.
