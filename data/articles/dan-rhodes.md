Dan Rhodes is a British magician who performs street and close-up magic for strangers and celebrities, filming their reactions.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "An Introduction To Dan Rhodes." from October 2015, a short showreel introducing his magic.

## The breakthrough
Dan grew with videos of tricks performed for footballers, celebrities and people on the street. His short clips performed especially well on TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

## Today
He continues to perform magic for the camera, and shares his work on Instagram and TikTok under @ItsDanRhodes and @DanRhodes.
