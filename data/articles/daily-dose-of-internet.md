Daily Dose Of Internet collects the best viral clips, plus forgotten gems, into short, fast-paced compilations.

## Video one
The oldest public video, "This Is An Air Fish" from April 2018, shows the format from the start: one surprising clip, explained in a few words.

## The breakthrough
The channel grew by finding genuinely surprising real-world videos and packaging them into tight compilations. It stresses that every clip is real and filmed by a real person, and that the original filmer's permission is always asked.

## Today
It continues to post compilations, and says it never features AI-generated content.
