Topper Guild makes prank and challenge videos with rapid editing designed to keep viewers watching. His channel grew into one of the biggest on YouTube in just a few years.

## Video one
His oldest public video, from October 2020, is "VIRAL TIKTOK PRANKS ON STEPHEN SHARER (HE WAS MAD)", made with another YouTuber when Topper was 18.

## The breakthrough
Topper grew rapidly in the early 2020s with short videos and challenges built on "retention editing", a fast style that keeps something happening every second. Commentators often compare his content to MrBeast's.

## Today
He ranks among the 20 or so most-subscribed channels in the world, with a mix of big challenge videos and Shorts.
