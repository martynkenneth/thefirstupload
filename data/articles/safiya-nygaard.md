Safiya Nygaard is known for curious, experimental videos about fashion and beauty, like mixing every nail polish together or testing strange products.

## Video one
Her oldest public video is "Safiya Nygaard Demo Reel 2014", a showcase of her on-camera work from her early career.

## The breakthrough
She first became known through BuzzFeed's LadyLike series, then launched her own channel. There, experiments like "Bad Makeup Science" and odd product tests found a big, loyal audience.

## Today
Safiya makes videos with her husband, Tyler, and describes her channel as "Always on a side quest".
