Oli, known as Mumbo Jumbo, is a British Minecraft creator famous for redstone, the in-game wiring system players use to build working machines. He is also known for his time on Hermitcraft and his famous moustache.

## Video one
His earliest uploads are no longer public. The oldest still on the channel is "Supercharged 4x4 Vault Door [Opens in under a second!]" from June 2013, a redstone build that shows what the channel is about.

## The breakthrough
Mumbo's clear, funny redstone tutorials made complicated Minecraft engineering approachable. Joining the Hermitcraft server brought him to a much larger audience.

## Today
He still makes redstone builds and Hermitcraft episodes, and aims to help viewers build things "you can show off to your friends".
