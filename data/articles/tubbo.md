Toby Smith, known as Tubbo, is a British Minecraft creator and streamer, known for his time on the Dream SMP and his friendship with TommyInnit.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "What It's like Starting out on Hypixel Skyblock" from September 2019.

## The breakthrough
Tubbo's Minecraft streams and his role on the Dream SMP made him one of Twitch's most-followed streamers.

## Today
He streams Minecraft and other games, and has been nominated several times for Best Minecraft Streamer at the Streamer Awards.
