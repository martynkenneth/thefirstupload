Nigel Braun, known as NileRed, makes chemistry videos that are both beautiful and surprising, like turning disposable gloves into grape soda.

## Video one
His first public video, "Making the Pharaoh's Serpent" from March 2014, shows a classic reaction where a small pellet grows into a long, snake-like column of ash.

## The breakthrough
Braun studied biochemistry at McGill University. His patient, carefully filmed experiments explain both the theory and the purpose of each step, which earned him a large audience. His goal, he says, is to capture "the natural beauty of chemistry".

## Today
NileRed releases long, ambitious projects on the main channel, with more experimental work on his second channel, NileBlue.
