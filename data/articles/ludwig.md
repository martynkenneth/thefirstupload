Ludwig Ahgren is a streamer and creator known for inventive game shows, big stream events and esports commentary.

## Video one
His oldest public video, "MVG Sandstorm Grand Final Power Outage" from April 2015, comes from his time in the Super Smash Bros. Melee community, where he was known as a commentator.

## The breakthrough
In 2021 Ludwig held a "subathon" where every new subscription added time to the stream. It ran for 31 days and made him the most-subscribed streamer in Twitch history at the time.

## Today
Ludwig won Streamer of the Year at the 2022 Streamer Awards, co-owns the esports organisation Shopify Rebellion, and streams on Twitch, with highlight videos on YouTube.
