FGTeeV is a family gaming channel starring a dad (Duddy), mum (Moomy) and their four children, who play games together with plenty of noise and silliness.

## Video one
Their oldest public video is "New Super Mario Bros U -- Mom Vs. Dad Coin Battle" from May 2013, a parents-versus-parents game session.

## The breakthrough
Family playthroughs of games like Minecraft, Roblox and horror titles built a big young audience, and FGTeeV became one of the best-known family gaming brands.

## Today
The family still plays new games together, and the brand has expanded into merchandise and books.
