LankyBox is a kids' entertainment channel run by two friends. Its colourful characters (Foxy, Boxy, Ghosty, Rocky and Thicc Shark) appear across gaming videos, animations and merchandise.

## Video one
The oldest video still on the main channel is "LANKYBOX K-POP CAR RIDE LIP SYNC" from March 2017, a lip-sync clip from long before the animated characters existed.

## The breakthrough
LankyBox found its audience with Roblox and other gaming videos, then created its own cast of characters. That turned the channel into a recognisable brand for young viewers.

## Today
The channel mixes gaming, animation and Shorts, and the characters have expanded into toys and merchandise.
