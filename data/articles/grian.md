Grian is a British Minecraft creator known for creative building, his role on the Hermitcraft server and creating the hugely popular Life Series.

## Video one
Grian's earliest videos are no longer public. The oldest still on his channel is "Building With Grian - Minecraft Tree Tutorial" from December 2014, a building tutorial in the style that first made his name.

## The breakthrough
Grian co-founded Wynncraft, the largest role-playing game built inside Minecraft. His building tutorials and humour grew his channel, and joining Hermitcraft in 2018 brought him to a huge shared audience.

## Today
He continues to play on Hermitcraft and runs the Life Series, a survival game where a group of creators share limited lives and lots of chaos.
