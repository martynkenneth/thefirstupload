JoshDub makes comedy videos in virtual reality, mostly chaotic adventures with his friends, which his channel sums up as "weird VR memes with the boys".

## Video one
His oldest public video is a VR climbing-game video with his friend Mullen, from May 2017, back when consumer VR headsets were brand new.

## The breakthrough
Silly VR games proved a perfect fit for short, funny videos, and JoshDub grew alongside VR gaming itself.

## Today
He continues to upload VR comedy and Shorts with his group of friends.
