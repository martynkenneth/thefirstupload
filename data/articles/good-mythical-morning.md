Good Mythical Morning is a daily comedy talk show hosted by Rhett & Link, two lifelong friends who call themselves "Internetainers". It is famous for food taste tests, games and celebrity guests.

## Video one
The oldest video on the channel is "The Bloody Door", from October 2008, an early comedy short from the years when Rhett & Link were making sketches and funny local commercials.

## The breakthrough
Rhett & Link built a following with comedic songs and sketches, and with their IFC series Commercial Kings. In 2012 they launched Good Mythical Morning, and its weekday-morning format made it one of YouTube's longest-running and most-watched shows.

## Today
GMM runs Monday to Friday, and their company, Mythical, produces several shows and a podcast, Ear Biscuits.
