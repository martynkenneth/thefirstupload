David Nagle, known as Nogla, is an Irish gaming creator known for funny-moments videos with friends, especially VanossGaming's crew.

## Video one
His oldest public video is a GTA 5 funny moments video from January 2014.

## The breakthrough
Group sessions in GTA V and other games with the Vanoss crew made Nogla one of Ireland's best-known YouTubers.

## Today
He thanks fans for "over 12 years of watching my friends and I making dumb videos".
