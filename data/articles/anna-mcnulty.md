Anna McNulty is a Canadian self-taught contortionist whose flexibility challenges made her YouTube's top Canadian creator of 2023.

## Video one
Her oldest public video is "ABC Dance/Cheer Challenge!", a collab from August 2016, made when she was a young teenager into dance and cheer.

## The breakthrough
Anna taught herself contortion and posted her progress and challenges. Her bendy stunts and positive personality took off on YouTube and other platforms.

## Today
She continues to post flexibility and challenge videos with friends.
