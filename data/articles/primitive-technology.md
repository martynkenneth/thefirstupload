Primitive Technology is John Plant's channel, in which he builds tools, huts and kilns in the Australian bush, using only natural materials and no talking.

## Video one
The first video, "Wattle and Daub Hut" from May 2015, shows him building a small hut from sticks, mud and leaves, completely from scratch.

## The breakthrough
His silent, patient videos, with strict rules of no modern tools (fire from a fire stick, axes from shaped stones), fascinated millions of viewers and inspired a wave of imitators.

## Today
He continues to release primitive-technology projects, each one showing a new skill built from the ground up.
