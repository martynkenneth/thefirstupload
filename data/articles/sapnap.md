Sapnap is a Minecraft creator and streamer, and a member of the Dream Team with Dream and GeorgeNotFound.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Minecraft, But It's Raining Cats And Dogs..." from October 2019, from right as the Dream Team's popularity was taking off.

## The breakthrough
As a founding member of the Dream SMP and part of the Dream Team's Manhunt-era videos, Sapnap grew a large following very quickly.

## Today
He streams and makes Minecraft videos, and has co-owned the esports organisation NRG since 2022.
