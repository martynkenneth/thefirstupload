Dream is a Minecraft creator who became one of the platform's fastest-growing channels at the end of the 2010s. For years he kept his face hidden behind a simple smiley-face logo, before a face reveal in 2022.

## Video one
He had been active online since 2014, but the oldest video still on his channel is "this cursed Minecraft video will trigger you..." from July 2019, a short meme-style clip from right before his rise.

## The breakthrough
Later in 2019 he launched Minecraft Manhunt, a series in which he tries to beat the game while friends hunt him down. It became a phenomenon. Dream also became known for Minecraft speedrunning, although several of his records were later overturned because of evidence of cheating.

## Today
His invite-only server, the Dream SMP, became a storytelling project that drew in many popular creators and a large fandom. He has also streamed on Twitch and released music.
