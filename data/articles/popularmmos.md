PopularMMOs is a long-running Minecraft channel known for mob battles, arena fights and modded adventures.

## Video one
Before Minecraft, the channel covered other games. Its oldest public video is a first look at RuneScape from May 2012.

## The breakthrough
Minecraft mod showcases and "Mob Battles", pitting the game's creatures against each other, became hugely popular with young gamers in the mid-2010s.

## Today
The channel still posts Minecraft content, describing itself as "Your source for epic Minecraft content!"
