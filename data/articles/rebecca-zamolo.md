Rebecca Zamolo is best known for the Game Master Network, a long-running mystery and puzzle series that turned her channel into a family franchise.

## Video one
Her oldest public video is a flash-mob wedding dance to Kesha's "Timber" from June 2014, from her background as a dancer and performer.

## The breakthrough
The Game Master storyline, a mix of escape-room puzzles, mysteries and cliffhangers, gave viewers a reason to return for every episode, and it grew into one of YouTube's most popular kids' series. She also built a large following on TikTok.

## Today
Rebecca makes videos with her husband, Matt, and their daughter, Zadie, and calls her fanbase the #ZamFam.
