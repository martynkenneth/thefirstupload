Colin Furze is a British inventor famous for building outrageous machines in his garden shed, many of which set Guinness World Records.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Mr blobby" from February 2007, an early clip from long before the hoverbikes and jet-powered go-karts.

## The breakthrough
A trained plumber, Furze built a reputation for dangerous, brilliant contraptions: the world's longest motorcycle, a 71 mph mobility scooter, a working hoverbike and a secret underground bunker beneath his garden. Several of them set world records.

## Today
He continues to build big projects, has collaborated with franchises like Star Wars and Assassin's Creed, and has appeared on TV in the UK and Germany. His channel promises "constant disregard to health and safety".
