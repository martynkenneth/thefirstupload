Kurtis Conner is a Canadian comedian, YouTuber and podcaster known for funny commentary videos on internet culture.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "HOT JOKES & COLD DOGS" from July 2014, a comedy video with friend Jacob Sharpe.

## The breakthrough
After starting out with comedy sketches, Conner's commentary videos took off in the late 2010s. He also performs stand-up and hosts podcasts.

## Today
He invites viewers to "become a citizen of KurtisTown".
