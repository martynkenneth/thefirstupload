Alan and Alex Stokes are identical twins whose prank and challenge videos have made them one of the biggest channels on YouTube.

## Video one
Each twin first built a following separately before combining forces on a shared twins account. The oldest video on that channel is a 2017 Instagram Q&A, a simple sit-down video answering fans' questions.

## The breakthrough
Their twin-swap pranks and challenge videos took off on YouTube and TikTok in the late 2010s. In recent years they found a second wave of growth with fast-paced short videos and high-concept challenges.

## Today
The twins have been named on Forbes' Top Creators lists and the TIME100 Creators list, which highlights the most influential people online.
