Beta Squad is a group of five British creators who make challenges, sketches and football videos together. It is one of the UK's most popular YouTube groups.

## Video one
The channel's first video, "INTRODUCING... Beta Squad" from February 2019, introduces the five friends as "five regular males in one alpha house".

## The breakthrough
Each member already had a following of his own. Together, their mix of chaotic challenges, food tests and sketches found a much larger audience, and the group became regulars at charity football matches.

## Today
Beta Squad releases big-budget challenges and collaborations with celebrities and athletes, and members continue to run their own channels.
