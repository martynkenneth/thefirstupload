Danny Gonzalez is a comedian and commentary YouTuber who first became known for short comedy sketches on Vine.

## Video one
His oldest public video is a compilation of his best Vines from October 2014, the year he started his channel.

## The breakthrough
When Vine closed in 2017, Gonzalez moved to YouTube full time. His funny commentary videos, along with comedy songs, built a big audience.

## Today
He runs several personal and group channels, mixing commentary, comedy and music.
