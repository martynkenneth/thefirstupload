Kai Cenat is one of the most popular livestreamers in the world, known for comedy streams, viral catchphrases and record-breaking subathons on Twitch.

## Video one
His channel's oldest video is literally titled "MY FIRST YOUTUBE VIDEO!! (Introduction)", uploaded in January 2018. It's a short introduction from a teenager starting out on YouTube.

## The breakthrough
Kai grew through comedy skits and the AMP collective, then exploded as a livestreamer. He helped popularise phrases like "rizz" and "Fanum tax", and his long-running subathons broke Twitch subscriber records.

## Today
He is the most-followed and most-subscribed streamer on Twitch and created Streamer University, a boot camp for aspiring content creators. His YouTube channel collects highlights and longer videos from his streams.
