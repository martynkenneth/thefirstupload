David Dobrik became one of YouTube's most popular vloggers with fast, four-minute-twenty-second vlogs full of pranks, stunts and surprise gifts, starring his friend group, the Vlog Squad.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Cool Intro" from February 2015, a short clip from when he was still mainly a Vine creator.

## The breakthrough
Dobrik had over a million followers on Vine before starting his vlog. His tightly edited videos, often surprising friends with cars, became some of the most-viewed creator content of the late 2010s.

## Today
He has voice-acted in The Angry Birds Movie 2, hosted TV shows including Discovery's Dodgeball Thunderdome, and continues to post vlogs.
