Chris Dixon, known as ChrisMD, makes football challenges, FIFA videos and vlogs, and is the most-subscribed YouTuber from Jersey.

## Video one
His oldest public video is a Call of Duty: Black Ops no-scope montage from February 2011.

## The breakthrough
ChrisMD grew with FIFA videos, then football challenges with famous players and creators.

## Today
He describes his channel as "Entertaining YouTube with challenges, a football and well-timed memes."
