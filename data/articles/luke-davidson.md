Luke Davidson is a young creator who makes upbeat comedy skits and short videos, with the aim, as he puts it, of putting "a smile on peoples faces".

## Video one
His oldest public video is "I HIT 1 MILLION FOLLOWERS ON TIKTOK!" from May 2020, celebrating his first big milestone on another platform.

## The breakthrough
After growing on TikTok, Davidson brought his short comedy to YouTube, where Shorts helped his channel grow quickly.

## Today
He continues to post skits and Shorts.
