Deji, also known as ComedyShortsGamer, is a British creator and the younger brother of KSI. He is known for comedy, gaming and his part in the influencer boxing era.

## Video one
His oldest public video, "Short: Winning The Game" from December 2011, is an early comedy short.

## The breakthrough
Deji grew with gaming and comedy videos, then became part of the influencer boxing boom of the late 2010s.

## Today
He continues to post videos, and his bio offers new subscribers "free chicken".
