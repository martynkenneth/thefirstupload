Brent Rivera was one of the first big stars of Vine, and he later built one of the most-subscribed entertainment channels on YouTube, with challenge and prank videos featuring a rotating crew of friends.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Brent's Cooking Show - Lava Cake" from May 2012, a homemade cooking video made when he was in his early teens.

## The breakthrough
Brent found fame on Vine with short comedy sketches. After Vine closed he committed fully to YouTube, where group challenges with his friends, including his sister Lexi and the Amp World house, drove huge growth.

## Today
Rivera has acted in film and TV and continues to release weekly videos, alongside followings on Instagram and TikTok.
