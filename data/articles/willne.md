Will Lenney, known as WillNE, is a British creator known for commentary videos about internet culture and, more recently, travel and challenge videos.

## Video one
His oldest public video is a FIFA pack-giveaway video from April 2012, from his gaming beginnings.

## The breakthrough
WillNE grew through funny commentary on online trends, then expanded into wider entertainment including travel and challenges.

## Today
He co-founded the iced coffee company Rodd's with his friend James Marriott. His bio jokes: "my upload schedule is really good i promise".
