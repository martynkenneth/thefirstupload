Vlad and Niki is a children's channel starring two brothers whose pretend-play adventures are watched by preschoolers all over the world. It is one of the most-subscribed and most-viewed channels on YouTube.

## Video one
The first video, from April 2018, is "Vlad and Nikita Play with Colored Blocks", a simple clip of the brothers building with giant toy blocks.

## The breakthrough
Simple stories, bright colours and little dialogue made the videos easy to enjoy in any language. The family, who emigrated from Russia to the US, expanded the brand into many channels dubbed into different languages.

## Today
Vlad and Niki now runs more than 20 channels in many languages, mixing live action, animation and music. The brand also includes games, toys and animated shows.
