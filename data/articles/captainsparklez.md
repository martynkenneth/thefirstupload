Jordan Maron, known as CaptainSparklez, was one of the first Minecraft stars, famous for Minecraft-themed songs and music videos.

## Video one
He started on YouTube in 2010 with Call of Duty videos. The oldest public video on this channel is "Your Captain is Speaking" from July 2010.

## The breakthrough
Minecraft music videos made with vocalist TryHardNinja, starting with "Revenge", were for a time the most-viewed Minecraft videos on YouTube. He reached a million subscribers in early 2012.

## Today
After 13 years he stopped uploading Minecraft gameplay to his main channel in December 2023. He now posts other games, vlogs and occasional music videos, with longer gameplay on CaptainSparklez2.
