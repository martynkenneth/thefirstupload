Charles White, known as penguinz0 or MoistCr1TiKaL ("Charlie"), is famous for his monotone voice and deadpan commentary on internet culture and video games.

## Video one
His oldest public video goes all the way back to August 2007: "gears of war jetpack glitch", a Gears of War glitch demonstration made when he was a teenager.

## The breakthrough
His commentary videos, calm takes on the internet's wildest stories delivered with dry humour, grew steadily through the 2010s and exploded as his Twitch streams took off around 2020.

## Today
White co-founded the esports organisation Moist Esports, is part of the music duo the Gentle Men, and still uploads commentary videos almost daily.
