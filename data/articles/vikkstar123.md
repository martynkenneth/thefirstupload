Vikram Barn, known as Vikkstar123, is a member of the Sidemen who started with Call of Duty and became known for gaming, challenges and DJing.

## Video one
His oldest public video, "MW2 INSANE Game Winning Killcam!" from June 2010, is a Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2 clip.

## The breakthrough
Call of Duty Let's Plays built his audience, and he later diversified into vlogs, challenges and collaborations as part of the Sidemen.

## Today
He continues to make gaming and group videos and also performs as a DJ. His bio reads: "It's the 123."
