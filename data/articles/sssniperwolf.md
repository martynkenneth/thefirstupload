Alia Shelesh, known as SSSniperWolf, became one of YouTube's most-subscribed female creators through gaming and, later, reaction videos.

## Video one
She first uploaded in 2011 under a different name, and started the SSSniperWolf channel in 2013. Its oldest video is "BO2 Fails + Funnies #1", a compilation of Call of Duty: Black Ops 2 clips from January 2013.

## The breakthrough
Call of Duty Let's Plays built her early audience, but her switch to reaction videos in 2017 made her one of the biggest channels on the platform. British Vogue later described her as one of the most recognisable gaming personalities online.

## Today
She continues to make reaction content. In 2023 she was widely criticised after an online dispute with another YouTuber and was temporarily demonetised by YouTube.
