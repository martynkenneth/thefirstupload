Dude Perfect is a group of five friends who met as roommates at Texas A&M University. Their trick-shot videos made them one of the most-watched sports channels on YouTube.

## Video one
Their first video, from April 2009, is literally titled "Our 1st Video!". It shows the group sinking long-range basketball shots in a backyard, the low-budget start of everything that followed.

## The breakthrough
The trick shots got bigger: off rooftops, from stadium upper decks and even from a helicopter. For each shot they made, they promised to sponsor a child through Compassion International, which helped the early videos spread. Recurring formats like "Stereotypes" and "Battles", where the members compete against each other, kept fans coming back.

## Today
Dude Perfect runs a full media company headquartered in Frisco, Texas, with its own TV-style show (Overtime), live tours, books and merchandise. They remain one of the most popular family-friendly channels on the platform.
