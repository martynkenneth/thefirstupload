Nick Amyoony, known as Nick Eh 30, is a Canadian streamer known for family-friendly Fortnite content and a relentlessly positive attitude.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "The Last Of Us Remastered Online: Bow Tips & Tactics" from December 2014, made while he was a student at Dalhousie University.

## The breakthrough
Nick began playing Fortnite Battle Royale in late 2017, and his streams took off so fast that he left university to stream full time. His no-swearing, positive style made him a favourite with younger players and parents.

## Today
He streams Fortnite daily and calls his community the #EhTeam.
