HowToBasic is an Australian comedy channel that looks like a how-to channel and is nothing of the sort. Its videos are chaotic, destructive gags, very often involving eggs.

## Video one
The first video, "How To Pick Up a Umbrella" from December 2011, set the template: a simple-sounding tutorial that turns absurd.

## The breakthrough
The channel took off in 2013 as viewers shared its baffling "tutorials". The creator never speaks or shows his face, which only added to the mystery.

## Today
HowToBasic is one of Australia's biggest channels. Its creator remains anonymous, and the channel still invites viewers to "post a video suggestion".
