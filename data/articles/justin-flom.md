Justin Flom is an illusionist and creator who performs magic with everyday objects, and is unusual among magicians for openly revealing how tricks are done.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Dancing Duck" from August 2006, from YouTube's very early days.

## The breakthrough
Flom performed on stage, opened for bands and appeared on the TV show Wizard Wars. During the COVID-19 pandemic his short videos took off on Facebook and YouTube, reaching billions of views.

## Today
He describes himself as "a creator, builder, comedian, father and sometimes a magician", and still breaks magic's "number one rule" by explaining the secrets.
