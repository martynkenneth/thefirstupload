Drew Gooden is a comedian and commentary YouTuber who first became famous on Vine for his "Road Work Ahead" clip.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Pre-Google", a comedy sketch with Gabriel Gundacker from February 2016.

## The breakthrough
After Vine closed, Gooden moved to YouTube, where his dry, funny videos about internet and pop culture found a large audience. He often collaborates with Danny Gonzalez, and the two toured together in 2019.

## Today
He won the Streamy Award for Commentary in 2021 and still posts regularly. His bio reads: "i am the only person who makes videos".
