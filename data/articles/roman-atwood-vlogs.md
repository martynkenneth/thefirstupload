Roman Atwood made his name with prank videos, then built a second huge audience with daily family vlogs. His sign-off was always "Smile more".

## Video one
The vlog channel's first video is "First Vlog - Pranks & Family" from August 2013, introducing a new side of Roman beyond his prank channel.

## The breakthrough
His prank videos had already earned more than a billion views. His daily vlogs about life with his family took off, and he became only the second YouTuber, after Germán Garmendia, to receive Diamond Play Buttons for two channels.

## Today
Atwood launched a podcast channel in 2022 and still posts family vlogs. He has also designed a board game, War and Pieces.
