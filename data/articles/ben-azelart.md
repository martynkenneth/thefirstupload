Ben Azelart makes fast-paced challenge and adventure videos, often with his friends and family. He is one of the most-subscribed young creators on YouTube.

## Video one
The oldest video on his channel is "THE START OF MY YOUTUBE!" from August 2016, made when he was a teenage skateboarder starting out on social media.

## The breakthrough
He first grew an audience with skateboarding and stunts, then joined the wave of creators making challenge videos with friends. His upbeat, high-energy format took off on both YouTube and short-form platforms.

## Today
Ben continues to upload challenge videos and shorts. His channel description promises "free cereal" to new subscribers, a fair summary of its playful tone.
