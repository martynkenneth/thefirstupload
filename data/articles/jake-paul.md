Jake Paul went from Vine comedian to Disney Channel actor, controversial vlogger and, most recently, professional boxer.

## Video one
He started on Vine in 2013 and launched his YouTube channel in May 2014. Its oldest public video is "Jake Paul Vine Compilation Part 1", collecting the short sketches that first made him famous.

## The breakthrough
Jake starred as Dirk Mann on the Disney Channel series Bizaardvark while vlogging daily with his Team 10 house. His vlogs made him one of YouTube's highest-paid creators.

## Today
Since 2018 Paul has focused on boxing, fighting former athletes and MMA fighters in heavily promoted events. He also runs businesses, including the brand W.
