Gustavo Tosta, known as Guga, is a Brazilian-American chef famous for steak experiments, especially dry-aging meat in unusual ingredients.

## Video one
His oldest public video, from July 2015, shows how to clean a Florida spiny lobster tail.

## The breakthrough
Guga's experiments, like dry-aging steaks in butter or cheese and then judging the result, became a huge hit, helped by his enthusiastic personality.

## Today
He continues to cook and experiment, with one simple request: "Subscribe if you love steak :)"
