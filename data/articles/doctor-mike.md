Mike Varshavski, known as Doctor Mike, is a practising family-medicine doctor who uses YouTube to explain health topics and debunk medical misinformation.

## Video one
His channel's first video, "WELCOME TO MY CHANNEL!" from April 2017, introduced him as a doctor who wanted to make medicine relatable and fun.

## The breakthrough
Varshavski first went viral on Instagram around 2015. On YouTube he found an audience by reacting to medical dramas, answering patient questions and fact-checking viral health claims, mixing real expertise with humour.

## Today
He still practises medicine in New York while producing videos with a full team, and remains one of the most-followed health creators online.
