Alexis, known as Quackity, is a Mexican creator and streamer known for Minecraft comedy and his role on the Dream SMP.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Toontown - The Typical HQ Officer" from June 2013, from the online game Toontown.

## The breakthrough
Quackity's Minecraft comedy and roleplay, especially on the Dream SMP, made him one of the platform's most popular Minecraft creators. He won Best Minecraft Streamer at the 2022 and 2023 Streamer Awards.

## Today
In 2025 he announced Dababel, a tool that helps people who speak different languages understand each other.
