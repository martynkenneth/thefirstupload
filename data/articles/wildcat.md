Wildcat, whose real name is Tyler, is a gaming creator known for funny-moments videos with friends.

## Video one
His oldest public video is a Call of Duty: Black Ops commentary on Nuketown from October 2011, titled "About Me".

## The breakthrough
Group gaming sessions full of jokes, often with other popular gaming creators, grew his channel through the 2010s.

## Today
