Niko Omilana is a British prankster and comedian, a member of Beta Squad, and a candidate in two real elections as part of his "Niko Defence League" movement.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Morgan Freeman Song", from August 2014, a comedy song from his teenage years.

## The breakthrough
Niko's prank and impersonation videos grew a big UK audience. He co-founded Beta Squad and has played in Soccer Aid and the Sidemen charity football matches.

## Today
Niko ran as an independent candidate for Mayor of London in 2021 and in the 2024 general election. He wasn't elected either time, but he won plenty of attention. He still uploads pranks and challenges.
