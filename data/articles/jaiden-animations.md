Jaiden Dittfach, known as Jaiden Animations, makes animated story-time videos about her life, school, games and her pet bird, Ari.

## Video one
Her channel launched in 2014, and the first video is "JaidenAnimations Intro!", a short animated hello from July that year.

## The breakthrough
Her heartfelt and funny animations, about everything from Pokémon to anxiety, built a huge following. She won the Streamy Award for animation in 2020 and became one of the best-known faces of YouTube's animation community.

## Today
Jaiden releases fewer, longer, carefully made videos, and has been open with her audience about her identity and mental health.
