Sierra & Rhia FAM is a family channel of short, kid-friendly stories, told by the children themselves, about everyday situations.

## Video one
The oldest public video is "Indoor Playground for Kids and Family" from February 2020, a simple day-out clip from before the channel found its format.

## The breakthrough
Short, family-safe stories, designed to be easy to understand in any language, took off with young audiences on YouTube Shorts. The channel says it once reached 2.6 billion views in a single week.

## Today
The family continues to post short stories for children and their families.
