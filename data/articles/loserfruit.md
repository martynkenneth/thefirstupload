Kathleen Belsten, known as Loserfruit or Lufu, is an Australian streamer and gaming creator best known for Fortnite.

## Video one
Her oldest public video is "I HATE THE META: LEGAL LEGENDS" from May 2013, from her early gaming days.

## The breakthrough
Loserfruit became one of Fortnite's best-known creators, with Let's Plays on her main channel, vlogs on Lufu and Fortnite Creative videos on another channel. She was also part of the Australian creator house Click House.

## Today
She streams on Twitch and runs several YouTube channels. Her main channel bio reads simply: "Hi."
