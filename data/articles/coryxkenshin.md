Cory Williams, known as CoryxKenshin, is famous for comedic horror game playthroughs and a loyal fanbase known as "the Samurai".

## Video one
He joined YouTube in 2009. The oldest video still public, "Girls in particular" from May 2009, comes from his very early days of talking to a camera, years before gaming became his focus.

## The breakthrough
Cory's reactions to horror games, from Five Nights at Freddy's to The Mimic, built a huge audience. He became known for taking long breaks and returning to massive excitement, and in 2021 he was ranked one of the top creators in the US.

## Today
He continues to upload horror playthroughs and other games. He has also expanded into writing, creating the manga series Monsters We Make.
