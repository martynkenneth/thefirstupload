Shane Dawson was one of the first people to become famous on YouTube. His career moved from comedy sketches to podcasts, films and long-form documentary series.

## Video one
Dawson began making videos in 2008, at 19. His earliest uploads were later deleted, and the version shown here, "KERMIT THE FROG & ME", is a copy re-uploaded to an archive channel in 2024.

## The breakthrough
His early sketches, playing original characters and celebrity impressions, earned hundreds of millions of views within two years. He later built a new audience with conspiracy-theory videos and multi-part documentary series about other YouTubers.

## Today
Some of his early sketches were later criticised for offensive content, including racial stereotypes, and in 2020 he apologised for them. He has posted far less since then.
