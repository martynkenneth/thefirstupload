Jasmin and James make short, feel-good videos as a couple, and have built one of the biggest Shorts audiences on YouTube.

## Video one
Their oldest public video is a Short from September 2021, set to the song "ili" by Troyboi.

## The breakthrough
Quick, cheerful skits and couple moments, made to "make you smile", spread quickly on YouTube Shorts and other short-video apps.

## Today
They call their fanbase the #JJFAM, and their channel's stated goal is the "road to 100M subscribers".
