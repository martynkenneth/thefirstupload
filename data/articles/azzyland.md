Azzy Bajrami, known as AzzyLand, is a Canadian creator who makes gaming, challenge and reaction videos. In 2019 she was the most-viewed female YouTuber.

## Video one
Her channel's first video is literally titled "MY FIRST VIDEO: Azzy's Intro To Cosplay and Gaming!", posted in May 2015.

## The breakthrough
Azzy's friendly, upbeat mix of games, challenges and reactions built a huge young audience in the late 2010s, peaking with 1.9 billion views in 2019.

## Today
She continues to upload reactions, games and challenges, and describes AzzyLand as a place where "everyone is treated with kindness and love".
