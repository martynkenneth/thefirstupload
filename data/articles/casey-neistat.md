Casey Neistat is a filmmaker whose daily vlogs in 2015 and 2016 changed vlogging on YouTube, bringing cinematic editing and storytelling to everyday life.

## Video one
His oldest public video, "Emergency Brake" from February 2010, is a short film made years before his daily vlog. Neistat was already an established filmmaker by then, with an HBO series and viral short films behind him.

## The breakthrough
His daily vlog, filmed in New York with drone shots, timelapses and his signature boosted-board commutes, inspired a generation of creators. He co-founded the video app Beme, later acquired by CNN.

## Today
Neistat founded 368, a space for creators to collaborate, and still posts occasional films and vlogs.
