Jessica Bravura, known as Aphmau, is one of the most popular Minecraft creators. She is known for long-running, family-friendly role-play series that play out like animated TV shows inside the game.

## Video one
Her oldest public video is "Minecraft Tekkit [Part 1] - Getting Started!" from August 2012, a standard modded-Minecraft Let's Play.

## The breakthrough
Aphmau found her audience by turning Minecraft into a stage for stories. Series like Minecraft Diaries built huge fanbases with recurring characters, voice acting and cliffhangers.

## Today
She continues to make role-play series and gaming videos, and has built a wider brand with merchandise and books.
