Turner Tenney, known as Tfue, is a streamer and former esports player best known for Fortnite Battle Royale.

## Video one
His oldest public video is a Call of Duty: Advanced Warfare match from November 2014.

## The breakthrough
Tfue became one of Fortnite's top players during its 2018 boom, with huge audiences for his streams and competitive play.

## Today
He still posts on YouTube, now with a mix of content beyond Fortnite.
