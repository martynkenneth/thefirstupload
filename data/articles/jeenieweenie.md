Jeenie.Weenie is a former flight attendant who makes comedy skits about travel, family and married life, and grew a huge following on TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

## Video one
Her oldest public video is "Tiktok's @Jeenie.Weenie Intro Q&A" from August 2020, introducing herself to YouTube after going viral on TikTok.

## The breakthrough
Relatable skits about flying, airports and family life, many drawn from her years as a flight attendant, made her a short-form star.

## Today
She makes travel and family skits, vlogs and beauty videos, and has started a kid-friendly channel. Her motto: "Catch flights, not feelings."
