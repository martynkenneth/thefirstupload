Arun Maini, known as Mrwhosetheboss, runs one of the largest tech channels in the world. He is known for making smartphone and gadget news genuinely entertaining.

## Video one
He started as a teenager in 2011. His oldest public video is a practical tutorial, "How to optimize your ZTE Blade", from April 2011, made for owners of a budget Android phone that was popular in the UK at the time.

## The breakthrough
He kept making tech videos through his university years, and his channel grew steadily with a mix of reviews, comparisons and explainers. His high-energy editing and big concepts, like testing phones in extreme conditions, set his channel apart.

## Today
Maini describes his mission as making "the most fun and useful tech videos on the planet". His channel covers every major phone launch and the industry stories behind them.
