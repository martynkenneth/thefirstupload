Chloe Ting is an Australian fitness creator and the most-subscribed fitness YouTuber in the world. She is known for free, follow-along workout programmes.

## Video one
Her oldest public video is "Spring Lookbook 2016", a fashion video, made before fitness became her focus.

## The breakthrough
Her free workout challenges, especially the "Two Week Shred", went viral during the COVID-19 lockdowns of 2020, when millions of people were exercising at home.

## Today
Chloe posts weekly workouts and healthy recipes, and offers her programmes through a free app.
