Mark Wiens is an American food and travel vlogger based in Bangkok, known for his enthusiastic street-food tours around the world.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Zanzibar Orange Peeler" from February 2009, a short clip from his early travels.

## The breakthrough
Wiens grew a huge audience with joyful, detailed street-food videos, from Thai curries to market stalls worldwide. His big reactions to good food became his trademark.

## Today
He posts two videos a week and also runs restaurants. As he puts it: "Let's go eat!"
