Preston Arsement, known as PrestonPlayz, makes family-friendly gaming videos and real-life challenges, mostly around Minecraft, Roblox and Fortnite.

## Video one
His current channel's oldest video, "BUT WHY!? - Castle Crashers: Part 1" from January 2013, is a co-op playthrough recorded with a friend, from the era when he was best known as TBNRfrags.

## The breakthrough
Preston grew with Minecraft minigames and challenge formats, then expanded into real-life videos with his family. His bright, upbeat style made him a favourite with younger gamers.

## Today
He runs several channels and describes his content as "fueled by fun, family, and faith".
