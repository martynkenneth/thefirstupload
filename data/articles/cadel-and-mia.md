Cadel and Mia are a couple who make short, lighthearted videos, and whose channel grew extremely quickly on YouTube Shorts.

## Video one
Their oldest public video is "OUR 1ST SUBSCRIBER!" from January 2025, celebrating their very first subscriber.

## The breakthrough
Daily Shorts made the channel grow to tens of millions of subscribers in a short time.

## Today
They post new Shorts daily, and their channel notes that it is "the ONLY Cadel and Mia account".
