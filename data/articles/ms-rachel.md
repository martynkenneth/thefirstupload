Rachel Accurso, known as Ms Rachel, is a teacher whose songs and learning videos for babies and toddlers have become a favourite with families around the world.

## Video one
Her oldest public video, "Bubble, Bubble Pop!" from February 2019, is a circle-time song for little ones.

## The breakthrough
Accurso, who holds master's degrees in music education and early childhood education, began the series after struggling to find good videos for her son, who had a speech delay. It took off during the COVID-19 pandemic and has been compared to Mister Rogers' Neighborhood.

## Today
Netflix began streaming Ms Rachel in 2025, and it became one of its most-watched children's shows. The series was nominated for Children's and Family Emmy Awards in 2026.
