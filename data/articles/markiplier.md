Mark Fischbach, known as Markiplier, built one of YouTube's most loyal audiences with Let's Plays of indie horror games, then turned that following into a career in filmmaking and acting.

## Video one
Markiplier started in 2012, and the oldest public upload on his channel is an "Amnesia Reaction Compilation" from May of that year. Amnesia: The Dark Descent was the game that defined his early channel, with nervous commentary and big jump scares.

## The breakthrough
His playthroughs of Five Nights at Freddy's in 2014 made him one of the most-watched gaming creators on the platform. He kept growing by mixing genres: comedy sketches, charity livestreams and the choose-your-own-adventure YouTube series A Heist with Markiplier.

## Today
He has written and directed In Space with Markiplier, starred in the audio drama series The Edge of Sleep, and co-hosts popular podcasts. In 2026 he released Iron Lung, a horror film he directed, wrote, produced and starred in.
