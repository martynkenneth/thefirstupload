Celine Dept is a football-loving creator whose short challenge videos have made her one of the fastest-growing channels of the 2020s.

## Video one
Her oldest public video is a Short from May 2023, "My 1st Subscriber?!", celebrating the very first person to subscribe.

## The breakthrough
Short, upbeat football challenges, posted almost daily, took off on YouTube Shorts. Her channel grew to tens of millions of subscribers in only a couple of years.

## Today
Celine posts new videos every day. Her channel introduces her simply: "Hi, my name is Celine & I love football!"
