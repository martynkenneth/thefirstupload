Evan Fong, known as VanossGaming, is one of the most influential gaming creators of the 2010s. He pioneered fast-edited comedy montages of friends playing GTA V, Garry's Mod and more.

## Video one
The oldest video on his channel is "IT DOESN'T MATTER! (SONG)", a music remix video from June 2012. He also produces music, and released the album In Pieces under the name Rynx.

## The breakthrough
Fong signed with the network Machinima early on and was regularly its most-viewed channel. His group videos with friends, each with their own avatar and running jokes, became a template for a whole style of gaming content.

## Today
Fong co-founded the entertainment company 3Blackdot and a record label, starred in animated shows like Alpha Betas, and still uploads gaming videos alongside his music.
