Infinite is one of two big channels run by Caylus Cunningham, known for reaction and gaming videos. His other channel, Caylus, is also on this site.

## Video one
The channel's oldest public video, from August 2016, is titled "Infinite Lists 1st video.. (do not watch)", from back when the channel was called Infinite Lists.

## The breakthrough
Reaction videos and list-style content grew the channel steadily, and Cunningham later added a second channel under his own name.

## Today
He describes himself as "the guy who looks at things", and continues to post reactions and gaming.
