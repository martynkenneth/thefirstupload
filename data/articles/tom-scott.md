Tom Scott is a British presenter who made short, fascinating educational videos about places, language, science and technology, filmed on location around the world.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Breakfast: Fortified With Iron" from June 2006, an early comedy clip from long before his educational work.

## The breakthrough
Scott first got media attention as a student for a parody government website. His channel took off after 2014 with Things You Might Not Know, along with series like Language Files and Amazing Places.

## Today
In January 2024, after ten years of weekly videos, Scott stopped regular uploads to take a break. He continues to work on projects including the Lateral podcast.
