Matt Larose is a parkour athlete and video editor who turns flips, stunts and clever editing into entertaining short videos.

## Video one
His oldest public video, uploaded in October 2010, is a parkour and free-running reel he filmed in spring 2007.

## The breakthrough
Larose combined real athletic skill with inventive editing, and his short stunt videos took off on YouTube Shorts and TikTok.

## Today
He describes his channel as turning "my passion for movement & editing into entertainment for you".
