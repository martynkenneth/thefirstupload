Michael Mandell, known as Law By Mike, is a Los Angeles attorney who explains everyday legal situations in short, entertaining videos.

## Video one
His oldest public video, from February 2021, is a Short about what to do when you get pulled over for speeding.

## The breakthrough
Clear, punchy explanations of people's legal rights, often acted out with a bit of drama, made his videos hugely popular on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. In 2025 he was listed among YouTube's top creators of the year.

## Today
Mandell runs his own law firm alongside the channel, and his videos are also dubbed into Spanish on separate channels.
