Collins Key started out as a magician on national TV and became a YouTube star with DIY challenges, pranks and family-friendly comedy.

## Video one
His oldest public video, from April 2012, is a card-flourishing ("cardistry") music video set to a dubstep remix, a sign of his early life as a sleight-of-hand performer.

## The breakthrough
Key was a finalist on the eighth season of America's Got Talent, toured as part of Demi Lovato's Neon Lights tour, and created an award-winning AT&T campaign called The Disappearing Girl. On YouTube he found a much bigger audience with gummy-food DIYs, pancake-art challenges and life hacks.

## Today
He continues to make comedy and challenge videos, often with his brother Devan.
