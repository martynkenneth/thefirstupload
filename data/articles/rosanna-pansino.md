Rosanna Pansino is the creator of Nerdy Nummies, a baking series that turns video games, films and pop culture into cakes and cookies. It made her one of YouTube's most popular food creators.

## Video one
Her oldest public video, "EARTH DAY" from April 2011, comes from when she was still finding her niche, months before Nerdy Nummies began.

## The breakthrough
Nerdy Nummies launched in 2011 with bakes inspired by games and films, and was perfectly timed for YouTube's gaming and fan audiences. Forbes named her the top food influencer in 2017.

## Today
Pansino has written cookbooks, launched baking product lines, starred in YouTube Premium's Escape the Night and hosted HBO Max's Baketopia.
