TommyInnit is a young British creator known for loud, chaotic Minecraft videos and streams. He was one of the breakout stars of the Dream SMP.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Tommy's First Video" from September 2018, made when he was 14.

## The breakthrough
Tommy's popularity exploded in 2020 through Minecraft collaborations and his role on the Dream SMP roleplay server, where his character became central to the storyline.

## Today
He runs more than a dozen channels and is the most-followed Minecraft creator on Twitch. His content has moved beyond Minecraft into comedy, interviews and travel. His channel bio reads: "I'm overrated but still good".
