Beau Miles is an Australian outdoorsman, educator and author whose thoughtful, quirky adventure films have a devoted following.

## Video one
His oldest public video, from May 2016, is a Q&A about kayaking across Australia's Bass Strait.

## The breakthrough
Films about offbeat challenges close to home made him a favourite for their humour and reflection.

## Today
He continues to make outdoor films that celebrate small adventures, described in his own playful bio.
