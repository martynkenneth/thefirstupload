Devon Rodriguez is a New York artist famous for drawing and painting portraits of strangers on the subway, then showing them the result.

## Video one
His oldest public video, from November 2019, is a live TV interview on Fox 5, from when his art was first getting attention.

## The breakthrough
Videos of Rodriguez sketching unsuspecting subway riders, and filming their reactions when he gives them the portrait, went viral on TikTok and then YouTube.

## Today
He continues to paint people in New York and share their reactions.
