Zach Choi is one of the biggest ASMR creators on YouTube, known for close-up, no-talking videos of cooking and eating.

## Video one
His oldest public video, "ASMR Eating Duck Fat French Fries (NO TALKING)" from August 2018, set the formula he still uses.

## The breakthrough
His crisp sound and simple format, with no talking and just food and close-up audio, built a huge global audience, since the videos need no translation.

## Today
Choi continues to post ASMR cooking and eating videos, and is also active on TikTok and Instagram.
