Tyler Blevins, known as Ninja, became the world's most famous streamer during the Fortnite boom of 2018.

## Video one
His oldest public video is titled "First video of many", posted in December 2011, when he was a competitive Halo player.

## The breakthrough
Ninja played Halo competitively for esports teams, then rose to fame with Fortnite in late 2017. In March 2018 he streamed Fortnite with Drake, Travis Scott and JuJu Smith-Schuster, breaking Twitch's viewer record at the time.

## Today
He streams Fortnite, Valorant and other games, and remains one of the most-followed streamers on Twitch.
