Joseph Garrett, known as Stampy or Stampylonghead, became one of the most-watched YouTubers in the world in 2014 with child-friendly Minecraft videos as his character Stampy Cat.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Journey - Starting My Journey [1]" from July 2012, the first part of a playthrough of the game Journey.

## The breakthrough
His long-running Minecraft series Stampy's Lovely World, full of storytelling and gentle humour, made him a huge favourite with young viewers. In 2015 he created Wonder Quest, an educational Minecraft series with Maker Studios and Disney.

## Today
Garrett ended Stampy's Lovely World in 2023, and in 2024 began working in game development.
