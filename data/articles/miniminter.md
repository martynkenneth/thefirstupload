Simon Minter, known as Miniminter, is a co-founder of the Sidemen, best known for FIFA videos, challenges and his friendship with KSI.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "FUT: Winning the gold cup (part 1)" from December 2012, a FIFA Ultimate Team video.

## The breakthrough
Minter grew with FIFA content, then diversified into vlogs, challenges and collaborations. As a Sidemen co-founder, he helped build one of the UK's biggest creator groups.

## Today
He co-owns several Sidemen businesses, co-founded the education non-profit M7 Education, and jokes that his channel is the place for videos with "KSI", "my girlfriend" and "the Sidemen" in the title.
