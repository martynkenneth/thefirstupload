Jesse Riedel, known as Jesser, is one of the biggest basketball creators on YouTube. His videos feature challenges, trick shots and appearances by professional players.

## Video one
His oldest public video, "Stalkatron 9000" from August 2013, is a short comedy clip from his early teens, long before basketball defined the channel.

## The breakthrough
Jesser grew with basketball challenges, games against friends in the 2HYPE crew and meetups with NBA stars. Pairing real skill with challenge formats made his channel a go-to for young hoopers.

## Today
He continues to upload basketball challenges and runs the apparel brand Bucketsquad.
