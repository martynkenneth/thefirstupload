Thinknoodles is a family-friendly gaming creator known for Roblox and Minecraft, often joined by his dogs Kopi and Kloï.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Club Penguin Cheats - Holiday Party 2011" from December 2011, from the era when Club Penguin was a hit with younger players.

## The breakthrough
Thinknoodles grew with friendly, easy-to-follow gaming videos for kids, moving from Club Penguin to Minecraft and then Roblox.

## Today
He still uploads family-friendly gaming videos and Shorts.
