Dan Middleton, known as DanTDM, is one of the most popular family-friendly gaming creators ever. He is best known for Minecraft adventures alongside his cartoon dog, Grim.

## Video one
DanTDM started in 2012 under the name TheDiamondMinecart. His first public video is the start of a Minecraft Pokémon mod series, "Pokemobs Adventure Mod #1 'The Journey Begins..'", from July 2012.

## The breakthrough
His Minecraft mod showcases and story series made him hugely popular with younger viewers. In 2016 Guinness World Records listed his channel for the most views for a dedicated Minecraft channel, and in 2017 he topped Forbes' list of highest-paid YouTubers.

## Today
Middleton has published a graphic novel, toured live shows around the world, voiced a role in the UK version of Ralph Breaks the Internet, and still uploads a wide range of games, from Minecraft to new indie titles.
