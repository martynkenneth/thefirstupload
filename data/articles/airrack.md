Eric Decker, known as Airrack, makes big, inventive challenge videos and is famous for gaining a million subscribers in a single year.

## Video one
His oldest public video, from July 2019, is "I Spent 24 Hours in the Real Stranger Things Town...", an early example of the stunt-driven vlogs that defined his channel.

## The breakthrough
Airrack set himself the goal of reaching a million subscribers in a year, and documenting that chase helped him get there. Ambitious stunts and celebrity collaborations kept the channel growing.

## Today
He continues to make challenge videos, and co-founded Creator Now, an education programme for aspiring YouTubers. His channel bio sums him up as a "Pizza Enthusiast".
