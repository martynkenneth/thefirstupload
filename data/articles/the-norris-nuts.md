The Norris Nuts are an Australian family of six siblings and their parents who make family vlogs and challenges.

## Video one
Their oldest public video is from the 2017 Kids' Choice Awards red carpet, featuring Sabre Norris, the young surfer who appeared on The Ellen Show.

## The breakthrough
After Sabre's viral TV appearances, the family's vlogs and challenges, with all six kids, found a big audience.

## Today
They post family vlogs and challenges, and also run a gaming channel.
