Ali Hassan, known as SypherPK, is a gaming creator and Twitch streamer best known for Fortnite.

## Video one
Before Fortnite, he played RuneScape. His oldest public video, "Epic Overkill - Runescape - Top 10 kills!" from September 2011, is a highlights reel from that game.

## The breakthrough
Fortnite Battle Royale made SypherPK one of its leading creators, known for detailed gameplay, tips and entertaining streams. He became one of the most-followed channels on Twitch.

## Today
He continues to make gaming videos and stream live on Twitch.
