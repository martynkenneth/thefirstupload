BadBoyHalo is a long-time Minecraft creator, known for his friendship with Skeppy, who is also on this site, and his role on the Dream SMP.

## Video one
His oldest public video, from June 2012, is the first part of a Minecraft Hunger Games series, from his early days on the platform.

## The breakthrough
Minecraft videos with Skeppy, often pranks at BadBoyHalo's expense, became hugely popular, and joining the Dream SMP brought him an even larger audience.

## Today
He continues to make Minecraft videos and runs his own Minecraft server, MunchyMC.
