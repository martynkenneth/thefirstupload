Jake Fellman is a self-taught 3D artist who makes short, polished animated comedy videos.

## Video one
His oldest public video is a Short from September 2020, "What does my logo mean?"

## The breakthrough
Fellman taught himself the 3D software Autodesk Maya in 2018 through YouTube tutorials. His visual comedy and polished renders found a huge audience on Shorts.

## Today
He continues to release short 3D animations.
