Alex Hirschi, known as Supercar Blondie, is an Australian presenter based in Dubai. She shows off the world's rarest and most expensive cars.

## Video one
Her oldest public video is "My Bentley ride with the Emirati Comedian - BinBaz!!!" from November 2015, from her early days filming car content in Dubai.

## The breakthrough
Short, enthusiastic videos on Facebook made her one of the fastest-growing automotive pages in the world in 2018. She expanded across Instagram and YouTube with tours of hypercars and futuristic vehicles.

## Today
Supercar Blondie is now a media brand with a team of presenters, and runs the car auction platform SBX Cars.
