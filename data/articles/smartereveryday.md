Destin Sandlin is an engineer who runs Smarter Every Day, a science channel built on curiosity and stunning slow-motion footage.

## Video one
The channel's first video goes back to January 2007: "How to Light a Bonfire with Rockets", which is exactly what it sounds like.

## The breakthrough
Sandlin's enthusiastic explorations of how things work, from bullet ballistics to how cats land on their feet, grew a loyal audience. In 2016 he was one of three YouTubers chosen to interview President Obama after his final State of the Union address.

## Today
He still makes Smarter Every Day videos and also runs a second channel and the No Dumb Questions podcast. His request to viewers: "Watch 2 videos. If you learn something AWESOME, please subscribe."
