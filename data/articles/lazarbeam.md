Lannan Eacott, known as LazarBeam, is one of Australia's biggest YouTubers. He is known for absurd humour, memes and gaming chaos.

## Video one
He began in 2014 by filming slow-motion demolition videos while working in his family's construction business. After his equipment was stolen, he started his current channel, and its first upload in February 2015 was a Madden NFL challenge video.

## The breakthrough
His Madden videos built a small following, but Fortnite in 2018 and Minecraft in 2019 made him a star. In 2019 he was one of YouTube's most-viewed creators worldwide.

## Today
LazarBeam's channel mixes gaming, vlogs and comedy. His bio simply states: "THIS CHANNEL FEATURES AUSTRALIAN MAN".
