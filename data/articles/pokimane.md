Imane Anys, known as Pokimane, is one of the most-followed women on Twitch. She is known for gameplay and chat streams, and for co-founding the creator group OfflineTV.

## Video one
Her oldest public YouTube video, from February 2016, explains "How to Pronounce 'Pokimane'" and where the name came from: a mix of Pokémon and her real name, Imane.

## The breakthrough
Pokimane grew through League of Legends streams, then Fortnite and Valorant. Collaborations within OfflineTV helped her reach a much wider audience.

## Today
She remains the most-followed female streamer on Twitch, and has launched her own snack brand, Myna.
