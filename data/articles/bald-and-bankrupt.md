Bald and Bankrupt is a travel channel run by Benjamin Rich, known for wandering into unusual places, especially in Eastern Europe and the former Soviet Union, and chatting with locals.

## Video one
The oldest public video is "I ❤️ India's Policewomen" from June 2018.

## The breakthrough
Rich's slow, unscripted walks through towns most tourists never see, and his conversations with the people he meets there, built a big global audience.

## Today
Russia was a major focus of the channel, but he was deported and banned from the country in 2022 after openly supporting Ukraine.
