Lucas and Marcus, the Dobre Twins, are dancers and creators who first went viral on Vine and built a family entertainment channel on YouTube.

## Video one
Their oldest public video is "What Are The Odds? w/ Jake Paul" from October 2015, made when Vine stars were teaming up on YouTube.

## The breakthrough
The twins rose to fame on Vine with dance and comedy clips. Their YouTube channel grew with pranks, challenges and family videos, often with their older brothers Cyrus and Darius as the Dobre Brothers.

## Today
The twins still run their own channel, while the four brothers also appear together on Dobre Brothers and the car-focused Dobre Cars.
