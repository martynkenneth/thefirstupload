Mehdi Sadaghdar, known as ElectroBOOM, is an electrical engineer who teaches electronics while regularly shocking himself and blowing things up on camera, on purpose (mostly).

## Video one
His first public video, "Don't worry, it's just ESD!" from April 2012, is about electrostatic discharge and features the comic mishaps that became his trademark.

## The breakthrough
Sadaghdar's blend of real engineering lessons and slapstick accidents struck a chord. Viewers learn how circuits work while laughing at the sparks, and his reactions to dangerous DIY videos became a popular series.

## Today
He continues to make educational comedy videos, describing his channel as a way to "subconsciously learn while being entertained".
