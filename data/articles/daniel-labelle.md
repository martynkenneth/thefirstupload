Daniel LaBelle makes short physical comedy videos with almost no dialogue, in the tradition of silent-film comedians and Mr. Bean.

## Video one
His oldest public video goes back to March 2009: a homemade "Kleenex Commercial", years before his comedy took off.

## The breakthrough
His skits, like how different people act in everyday situations, told through exaggerated movement, became hugely shareable because they work in any language. He cites Rowan Atkinson and the Three Stooges among his inspirations.

## Today
LaBelle continues to make short comedy videos and compilations, with the stated aim of "uplifting you and giving you smiles".
