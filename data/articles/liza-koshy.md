Liza Koshy is a comedian and actor whose quick-fire, pun-heavy sketches made her one of YouTube's biggest stars of the late 2010s.

## Video one
Her oldest public video, from July 2015, is an introduction titled "SUP, I'M LIZA THE LITTLE BROWN GIRL", from soon after she moved from Vine to YouTube.

## The breakthrough
Koshy's sketches and characters grew her channel rapidly, and in 2016 she interviewed Barack Obama for YouTube. Acting roles followed in Boo! A Madea Halloween and the Hulu series Freakish.

## Today
She co-created and starred in the YouTube Premium show Liza on Demand, hosted Nickelodeon's Double Dare, and starred in Netflix's Work It.
