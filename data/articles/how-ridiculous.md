How Ridiculous is a trio of friends from Perth, Australia, known for trick shots and for dropping heavy objects from a 45-metre tower onto creative targets.

## Video one
Their first video, "How Ridiculous?! Basketball Shots" from September 2009, is a set of long-range basketball trick shots in the same spirit as early Dude Perfect.

## The breakthrough
The group set several trick-shot world records, then found their signature format: dropping bowling balls, anvils, giant darts and even cars onto everything from trampolines to pools of oobleck.

## Today
How Ridiculous keeps experimenting with drop tests and trick shots, and is also popular on TikTok. As their bio puts it: "3 Australians having fun."
