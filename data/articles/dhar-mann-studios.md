Dhar Mann Studios makes short scripted dramas with a moral lesson, often about kindness, honesty or standing up for others. It is one of the biggest video studios built on YouTube.

## Video one
Dhar Mann founded the studio in 2018. Its oldest public video, "Before Deciding To Give Up On All Your Goals" from May 2018, comes from its early motivational phase.

## The breakthrough
The studio shifted from motivational clips to short morality plays with actors. Its videos passed 11 billion total views by 2023, and it grew to around 200 staff at its studio in Burbank, California.

## Today
In 2026 the studio signed deals to produce programming for Samsung TV Plus, Fox Entertainment and Disney.
