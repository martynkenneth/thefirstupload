Zach Hsieh, known as ZHC, is an artist who turns custom paint jobs and drawing challenges into big-budget YouTube videos, often giving the finished pieces away.

## Video one
He created his first channel in 2013 while studying at the School of Visual Arts in New York. His current channel's oldest video is titled simply "My First Video", from December 2016.

## The breakthrough
Hsieh began with art tutorials, then switched to custom art for other creators and celebrities: painted cars, consoles, rooms and more. He passed a million subscribers in 2019 and grew rapidly after that.

## Today
ZHC continues to make art challenges and custom pieces, along with gaming videos and Shorts.
