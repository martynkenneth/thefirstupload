Kris Collins, known as Kallmekris, is a Canadian comedian known for short sketches where she plays a whole cast of characters. She is one of the most-followed creators from Canada.

## Video one
Her YouTube channel began in 2020. Its oldest video is a simple "Q&A" from August 2020, posted months after she went viral on TikTok.

## The breakthrough
Collins was working as a hairstylist when she joined TikTok in April 2020, during the COVID-19 pandemic. Her character skits exploded there, and she brought them to YouTube.

## Today
Alongside skits, her channel now features true-crime deep dives. In 2025 she directed her first feature film, the found-footage horror House on Eden.
