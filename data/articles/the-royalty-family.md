The Royalty Family is a Los Angeles family channel run by Andrea Espada and Ali Saleh, featuring their children in challenges, pranks and family vlogs.

## Video one
The oldest video on the channel, "SUNDAY FUNDAY!!!!" from October 2017, is a casual family vlog with Andrea and her son Ferran.

## The breakthrough
Upbeat family challenges and everyday moments with the children helped the channel grow quickly. The family describes itself as a modern blended family that loves "fashion, comedy and culture".

## Today
They continue to post weekly family videos.
