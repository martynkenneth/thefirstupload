Ranboo is a Minecraft creator who rose to fame on the Dream SMP, known for an anonymous identity and a black-and-white character design.

## Video one
The oldest public video on the channel is "I decided to play skywars, it was a nightmare" from August 2020.

## The breakthrough
Ranboo joined the Dream SMP in late 2020 and quickly became one of its most popular members, growing a large audience within months.

## Today
He continues to stream and make videos. His bio reads: "Every upload is another blessed viewer experience."
