Drew Binsky is a travel creator who has visited every country in the world, telling stories about people and cultures along the way.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "Learn to Speak Czech!" from July 2012, from his early travels.

## The breakthrough
His short, people-focused travel stories took off on Facebook and YouTube. He completed his goal of visiting every United Nations-recognised country in October 2021, in Saudi Arabia.

## Today
He wrote the travel book Just Go and continues to tell stories about "the world's most interesting people, hidden communities, and remarkable cultures".
