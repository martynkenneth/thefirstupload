Unbox Therapy is a tech channel run by Lewis Hilsenteger, known for slick unboxings of the newest phones and gadgets.

## Video one
The oldest public video, from December 2010, is an unboxing and test of the Apogee One audio interface.

## The breakthrough
His clean, close-up unboxing style made the channel one of the biggest in tech. In 2014 his video bending an iPhone 6 Plus by hand helped launch the "Bendgate" story.

## Today
Unbox Therapy still covers new phones and unusual gadgets. As the channel puts it: "Where products get naked."
