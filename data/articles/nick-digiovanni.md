Nick DiGiovanni is a chef and creator who turned a run on a TV cooking competition into one of the biggest food channels on YouTube.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "My Final Dessert On MasterChef" from October 2019, a look back at the dessert he made on the show, where he was a finalist.

## The breakthrough
Short, punchy cooking videos on TikTok and YouTube Shorts made him a star. He became known for over-the-top dishes, collaborations with other big creators and attempts at food world records.

## Today
DiGiovanni continues to make cooking videos and challenges, from simple recipes to giant record-breaking dishes.
