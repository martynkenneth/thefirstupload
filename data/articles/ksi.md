KSI is one of Britain's biggest internet stars. He has moved from gaming to music, celebrity boxing and business, and co-founded the Sidemen.

## Video one
His first public video, "LOOKING THROUGH SPACE" from April 2008, was posted to an early channel called JideJunior, years before KSI existed. In 2009 he registered KSIOlajidebt and built his audience with FIFA commentary videos.

## The breakthrough
His energetic FIFA videos and comedy made him one of the UK's top YouTubers in the early 2010s. He branched into music, with "Lamborghini" becoming his first hit in 2015, and later headlined huge influencer boxing events, including two fights against Logan Paul.

## Today
KSI co-founded Misfits Boxing and the drinks brand Prime. He releases chart-topping music and has been a judge on Britain's Got Talent since 2025.
