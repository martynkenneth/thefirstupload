Peet Montzingo is a creator and musician known for videos with his family, all of whom have dwarfism, along with music and ghost hunting.

## Video one
His oldest public video is a cover of "Back to You" by Louis Tomlinson and Bebe Rexha, recorded with a friend in July 2017.

## The breakthrough
His videos featuring his family, often funny and warm, became popular on TikTok and YouTube.

## Today
Peet makes a mix of videos and music, and has recently become a children's author.
