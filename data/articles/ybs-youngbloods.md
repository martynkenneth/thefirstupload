YBS Youngbloods is a crew of friends led by Brodie Moss who share spearfishing, fishing and ocean adventures, with a focus on being "selective and sustainable".

## Video one
The oldest public video is "YBS Liquid Cinema Ep 1 - Hunting On A Heartbeat" from May 2014, the start of a series of underwater hunting films.

## The breakthrough
Their underwater footage and adventures, from spearfishing to cooking their catch, built a big audience around the YBS lifestyle.

## Today
They continue to share ocean and nature adventures with fans around the world.
