Lachlan Power is one of Australia's biggest gaming creators. He is best known for Fortnite, and was the first Australian gaming creator to reach 10 million subscribers.

## Video one
He registered his channel in 2013 under the name CraftBattleDuty. Its oldest video is the first episode of a Pixelmon (Pokémon mod) server Let's Play from March 2013.

## The breakthrough
After years of Minecraft, Battlefield and Call of Duty, Fortnite in 2017 turned Lachlan into a star. He passed 10 million subscribers in January 2019.

## Today
He founded the lifestyle brand and gaming organisation PWR, and his channel bio admits he plays "a bunch of video games including Fortnite (Just kidding its pretty much Fortnite)".
