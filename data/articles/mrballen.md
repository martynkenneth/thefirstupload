John Allen, known as MrBallen, is a former US Navy SEAL who tells true crime and mystery stories in a calm, gripping style.

## Video one
His oldest public video is "What I saw in my room still haunts me", from March 2020, one of his earliest story videos.

## The breakthrough
His stories, told sitting at a desk, often about unsolved mysteries or survival, took off on TikTok and then YouTube. He gives the people involved respect rather than sensationalising them.

## Today
He co-founded Ballen Studios, and his podcast, MrBallen Podcast: Strange, Dark & Mysterious Stories, is one of the most popular in its genre.
